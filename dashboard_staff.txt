Build a STAFF-FACING desktop dashboard for a physical vehicle rental store
called "RentWheels". Staff use it at the counter to watch sales and to see
walk-in and online rentals. It is NOT customer-facing. The style is modern,
minimal and card-based. It has very large rounded corners, airy spacing,
big numbers, and a warm paper-like feel.

=====================================================================
1. DESIGN TOKENS
=====================================================================
BG            #F1E8D8   page background
NAVBAR        #FFFFFF   sidebar
TEXT          #1A1A1A   primary text on light surfaces
MUTED         #8A8175   secondary text, captions, axis labels
ACCENT        #D8CCBB   ticks, chips, dividers
BORDER        #D8CCBB   1px outlines
SURFACE       #EEEAE1   icon-button backgrounds, active nav pill
SURFACE_HOVER #E6E1D6   hover state
INK           #171717   dark cards
PAPER         #FFFFFF   light cards, and text on dark cards

Extra tints derived from the palette (use only where the palette has no value):
INK_RAISED    #232220   pills/buttons sitting on dark cards
INK_LINE      #3A3733   outlines on dark cards
Warm chart tones: TAN #B8A68A, BROWN #8A6F4E, CREAM = BG #F1E8D8

Rules: no purple, no blue gradients, warm neutrals only. Tiny semantic dots
(green, amber, blue) are allowed only on status/payment pills.

Typography: one geometric sans (DM Sans, Inter or Manrope).
- Page title: 32px, light/regular weight, TEXT
- Card titles: 28px regular on light cards; 18px regular on dark cards
- Hero numbers: 56-64px, medium weight
- Body/labels: 14-15px
- Captions/axis labels: 11-12px, MUTED
Numbers use tabular figures so digits align.

Shape: card radius 28px, card padding 24-28px, gap between cards 16px,
pills fully rounded (999px), small icon buttons 40x40 with 12px radius.

=====================================================================
9. POLISH AND BEHAVIOR
=====================================================================
- Light cards: PAPER with a 1px BORDER outline and no shadow (or a very
  faint 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)).
- Dark cards: flat INK, no border.
- Icon style is consistent: thin outline, 1.5px stroke, rounded caps.
- Interactive elements use SURFACE_HOVER (on light) or INK_RAISED
  lightened by 6% (on dark) for hover.
- Motion: on load, curves draw in over ~600ms and heatmap cells fade in
  with a short stagger. Hover transitions are 120ms.
- Hierarchy: big numbers first, labels second, MUTED captions last.
  Whitespace stays generous.


  IMPLEMENTATION: PySide6. Use QSS for the cards, pills and sidebar, based on
the token constants above. Draw the sigmoid curves, the heatmap, the tick
ruler and the sales chart as custom QWidgets with QPainter, using
antialiasing and QLinearGradient for the fades. Use QGraphicsDropShadowEffect
only if a shadow is needed. Use a QGridLayout for the card grid. Keep the
data in a separate model/service layer so the widgets only receive plain
lists and dicts.
